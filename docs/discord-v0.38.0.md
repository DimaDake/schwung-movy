## Movy v0.38.0: generators, freeze, Ableton Link

**Generators.** Hold a shortcut in Track view and Movy writes new takes that fit the scene, the chords the other tracks play included:
- **Smart Quantize** (Shift + hold Step 16): six takes on a recorded clip, from TIGHT (on the groove, only clashing notes moved) to ones that develop it.
- **Variation** (Shift + hold Step 14): turns your A part into a B. Six options, closest first, and chords stay chords, drums keep their pads.
- **New Clip** (Shift + hold Step 14 on an empty track): a part from nothing, like CHORDS, PAD, BASS, ARP, MELODY or RIFF, or a whole drum kit.
- **Smart Double** (Shift + hold Step 15): doubles the loop with a new ending, a turnaround, fill or pickup, and leaves the first half as played.

Pick a take with the jog, shape it with the knobs, edit it with the pads, and undo your way back if you don't like it.

**Freeze a track.** Shift + a track button, then FREEZE. The track is recorded into loops and its synth and effects unload, so it sounds the same and costs almost no CPU. Clips, scenes and the mixer keep working. Thanks to **ldap** for building it!

**Ableton Link.** Settings → LINK. Movy shares tempo and Play/Stop with Live over WiFi or USB-C, and every track, send and the master shows up in Live as its own audio input to record.

**Also new:** a Ducker can duck one track to another track's notes, the built-in speaker gets Move's own EQ, and the Note length knob ends at FIT.

**Fixed:** clicks while turning a track's volume or editing a heavy Set, a Set opening for a second at default sounds, Virus Sets stuck on "Booting DSP", and a module that won't load now says so.

**New manual**, a website with screenshots: https://dimadake.github.io/schwung-movy/
**Install or update in one command**, Schwung included: https://dimadake.github.io/schwung-movy/install/

Built against **Schwung 1.9.4**. The installer updates an older Schwung for you.
